### Assessment Tools

**PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire)**
- Developed by Drs. Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B.W. Williams, Kurt Kroenke
- Validated depression screening tool
- Public domain

**GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale)**
- Developed by Dr. Robert L. Spitzer et al.
- Validated anxiety screening tool
- Public domain

**PCL-5 (PTSD Checklist for DSM-5)**
- Developed by National Center for PTSD
- PTSD symptom assessment
- Public domain

**MDQ (Mood Disorder Questionnaire)**
- Developed by Dr. Robert M.A. Hirschfeld et al.
- Bipolar disorder screening
- Validated in multiple studies

**PSS-10 (Perceived Stress Scale)**
- Developed by Sheldon Cohen et al.
- Stress assessment tool
- Widely validated

### Therapy Techniques

**Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**
- Based on work by Aaron T. Beck, M.D.
- Evidence-based cognitive restructuring
- Behavioral activation techniques

**Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)**
- Developed by Marsha M. Linehan, Ph.D.
- Mindfulness and distress tolerance skills
- Emotion regulation techniques

**Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)**
- Developed by Steven C. Hayes, Ph.D.
- Psychological flexibility
- Values-based action

**Mindfulness-Based Interventions**
- Based on work by Jon Kabat-Zinn, Ph.D.
- Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)
- Body scan and meditation practices

This platform was created with the belief that **mental health resources should be free, accessible, and evidence-based**. It's inspired by:

- The need for accessible mental health information
- The power of open-source collaboration
- The importance of reducing mental health stigma
