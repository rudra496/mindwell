# SUST Innovation Hub Awards Pre-Seed Funding to Team SignTalk — From Student Project to Startup

Moving from prototype to startup requires more than technical ambition. It needs support, mentorship, and early-stage funding. Through SUST Innovation Hub, Team SignTalk received pre-seed backing that helped us take this exact step.

## Why pre-seed support matters

Student teams often stop at “demo-level success” because they lack resources for product refinement, user pilots, and operational structure. Pre-seed support helps close that gap.

For SignTalk, this phase supports:

- Hardware refinement and reliability improvements
- Better UX for real-world usage
- Pilot planning and stakeholder engagement
- Early startup readiness (documentation, roadmap, outreach)

## The role of SUST Innovation Hub

SUST Innovation Hub has become a key enabler for student entrepreneurship by creating a bridge between campus innovation and practical venture development.

This ecosystem-level approach is important. It signals that innovation should not end in competitions; it should continue toward deployable, impact-oriented solutions.

## From project identity to startup identity

This transition changed how we think about product-building:

- “Can we build it?” became “Can people use it consistently?”
- “Does it work in lab conditions?” became “Does it survive real contexts?”
- “Is it innovative?” became “Is it useful, maintainable, and scalable?”

That mindset shift is the core value of pre-seed stages.

## Forward plan

With pre-seed momentum, we are focusing on:

1. Robust gesture recognition under varied usage patterns
2. Better multilingual speech output options
3. Field-informed design updates from user interactions
4. Stronger partnerships for pilot execution
