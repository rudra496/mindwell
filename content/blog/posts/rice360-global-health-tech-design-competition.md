# SUST Team Qualifies for Rice360 Global Health Tech Design Competition — A Milestone in Assistive Technology

Qualifying for an international health-tech platform is about more than one event result. It signals that a student team’s process, rigor, and relevance are recognized globally. Our SUST team’s qualification journey for the Rice360 Global Health Tech Design Competition was exactly that kind of milestone.

## What Rice360 represents

Rice360 programs focus on practical global health engineering: build technology that addresses real constraints in low-resource settings. That mindset aligns closely with our own work in assistive technology.

For student innovators, Rice360 is valuable because it rewards:

- Problem relevance
- Technical feasibility
- User-centered design
- Evidence of iterative prototyping

## Our qualification journey

Our preparation required tight execution across concept development, prototype communication, technical validation, and concise storytelling. We focused on clearly defining the user pain point and showing why our solution had practical impact.

Three process lessons stood out:

1. **Context-first framing beats feature-first pitching.** We led with user need and implementation environment.
2. **Documentation quality matters.** Judges evaluate not only innovation potential but also design clarity.
3. **Iteration visibility builds credibility.** Showing what changed between prototypes demonstrates engineering maturity.

## Why it matters for SUST and Bangladesh

This qualification contributes to a broader narrative: Bangladeshi university teams can compete meaningfully in global health and assistive technology when they combine technical depth with social relevance.

For SUST students, it creates a practical benchmark—international participation is possible with disciplined execution, collaboration, and mission-driven product thinking.

Our work across projects like SignTalk and other accessibility-focused builds reflects a consistent direction: technology that expands inclusion and dignity.
