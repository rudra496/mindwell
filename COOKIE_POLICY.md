# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-19

MindWell is designed with a privacy-first approach.

## Do we use tracking cookies?
No. MindWell does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## What we may use
- Essential technical storage (such as local browser storage) to support core functionality, for example language preference or accessibility settings.
- Security-related mechanisms necessary to keep the service stable and safe.

## Your control
You can clear browser storage at any time from your browser settings. Some features may reset if you do this.

If you have questions about privacy or cookie-related practices, contact: contactmindwellorg@gmail.com
