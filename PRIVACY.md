# Privacy Policy – MindWell

MindWell is an **educational, open-source mental health support platform** built with a strong focus on privacy, safety, and transparency.

---

## 1. Scope and Purpose

MindWell is designed to provide **educational information, self-reflection tools, and wellbeing resources**.  
It is **not a medical service** and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare.

---

## 2. Authentication

MindWell uses **Google (Gmail) authentication via Firebase** for **limited features only**, such as:

- Community participation
- Abuse and spam prevention
- Platform safety moderation

Authentication is **not required** to access core educational features.

Personal identifiers such as **name, email address, or profile photo are never publicly displayed or shared**.

---

## 3. Data Collection and Storage

### a. Local (Client-Side) Data
Whenever possible, user-generated data such as:
- Mood tracking
- Journaling
- Self-assessment results

is stored **locally on the user’s device** using browser storage (e.g., IndexedDB).

### b. Firebase Storage
For features that require moderation or shared interaction:
- Community posts and safety-related metadata may be stored in **Firebase Firestore**
- This data is used **only to operate the platform safely**

MindWell does **not sell, rent, or monetize user data**.

---

## 4. Use of Data

Any data processed by MindWell is used **solely for**:
- Providing educational and wellbeing-related features
- Preventing misuse, abuse, or spam
- Maintaining a safe community environment

MindWell does **not** use data for:
- Advertising
- Behavioral tracking
- Profiling
- Marketing analytics

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

MindWell uses **Firebase services** strictly for:
- Authentication
- Limited data storage required for safety and moderation

No third-party services are used for advertising or user tracking.

---

## 6. Privacy and Anonymity

- No public user profiles are shown
- No real names or emails are visible to other users
- No cookies or advertising trackers are used
- Anonymous usage is supported for core features

---

## 7. Medical and Safety Notice

MindWell is **for educational purposes only**.  
It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

In situations involving crisis or risk of harm, users are directed to **professional and emergency resources**.

---

## 8. User Responsibility

Users are encouraged to:
- Use MindWell as a **supportive and educational tool**
- Seek help from licensed professionals when needed
- Avoid relying solely on digital tools for serious mental health concerns

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform evolves.  
Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated revision date.
